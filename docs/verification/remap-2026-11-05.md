changed 114, of about 1,680 rules on each side.

Read:

- Whole-run totals are stable: 0.4 % on all candidates, about 2 % within a
  nature. A change in a whole output's rule count beyond a few percent is
  a real one; below it, one pair of runs cannot tell. This is one pair:
  the spread is a floor, not a bound.
- Rule identity is not stable. About half of the rules have no match in
  the other run, even under stable ids, because B had no prior output to
  take ids from and the reduce rewrites the requirements. A `diff` between
  two remaps therefore reports mostly noise in added, removed and renamed;
  compare counts, not rule lists.
